# Characters for v2: look, lore hooks, build notes (DRAFT to fine-tune)

Source: the existing painted art in `3d/art/sprites/` (contact sheet viewed 2026-11-12) plus the NPC lines and abilities already in `3d/index.html`. "FROM GAME" = already in the old game. "PROPOSAL" = my idea, change freely. Heights are metres; the hero is 1.83.

Build rule for all of them: same jointed rig as the hero, 4.5 heads tall, chunky limbs, flat colours, one **signature silhouette cue** and one **signature prop** each so they read at phone size from the high camera.

## The player: the Astronaut ("Frankie"? see questions)
- FROM GAME: cream suit, orange shoulders/belts, olive boots, blue knee pads, backpack with two tanks, short black hair, calm tired face, an eye patch on the chest panel.
- v2 status: built (placeholder face).
- PROPOSAL: keep the suit as the brand mark. Movement personality: steady, a little heavy, no showing off.

## The crew (the three you play alongside)
- **Christopher** (1.82): FROM GAME: black beanie, sunglasses, full beard, olive flight jacket with a NASA-style patch, cargo pants, one knee brace, **a cane**, duffel bag. Line: "It's not mine. It's ours." Ability: *refill*. PROPOSAL: slow, deliberate walk with the cane tapping; the only character who never runs. Palette olive, black, one orange patch. Cue: beanie + beard silhouette. Prop: duffel.
- **Juan Carlos** (1.72): FROM GAME: beard, rust shirt over a white tee, one arm in a sling holding a blue jacket, a coil of cable, a small screwdriver. Line: "You're one of us now." Ability: *invisible*. PROPOSAL: quick, light-footed, always glancing around; when he uses the invisible ability he goes translucent with a shimmer. Palette rust, sage, denim. Cue: sling + cable coil.
- **Eduardo** (1.76): FROM GAME: long dark hair, glasses, grey work shirt and grey trousers (the "plain" one). Line: "Hold the line." Ability: *cool*. PROPOSAL: planted stance, arms crossed when idle, barely moves unless he must. Palette greys with a single bright accent (suggest lilac, the colour his dialogue already uses). Cue: long hair + glasses.

## Neighbourhood NPCs (the quest-givers in the old game)
- **Pancho** (1.75): FROM GAME: weathered cap and glasses, work shirt, dirty apron printed "YOU BEST BELIEVE" with a UFO. Lines about foil hats and flying saucers. The foil-hat vendor, a lore comic relief. PROPOSAL: stands behind a cart with a small foil-hat rack; leans in when you approach.
- **Pancho's Father** (1.70): FROM GAME: older man, grey hair, blue cardigan over a white shirt, dark trousers, slippers. Line: "Frankie... the sky is a cage. I've carried heavier." He is the **power/refill** source ("Hold still, son. This is going to hum."). PROPOSAL: sits on a crate or porch step; a soft glow when the hum charges you.
- **The Kid** (1.30): FROM GAME: San Diego Padres cap, green backpack, white tee, jeans, red sneakers, a stack of cards, a fruit basket. Takes the CARDS. Line: "You don't even watch sports." PROPOSAL: bouncy, fidgety idle; runs to meet you.
- **Luz** (1.68): FROM GAME: cream blazer, rust top, green trousers, brown boots, a small radio/recorder in hand. Lines: "We never needed proof. We needed medicine." / "I was the first to identify the hum." PROPOSAL: calm, upright, the lore keeper; a faint ring of sound waves around her radio.

## The pressure (what you avoid or take down)
- **The Agency suit** (1.85): FROM GAME: black suit, white shirt, black tie, neat dark hair, no emotion. Line: "We were just in the neighborhood. Wanted to check on our guys." Role in v2: **the patrol agent**. Replace my placeholder navy agent with this look (pale cone, "!" when alerted).
- **The thug** (2.15): FROM GAME: huge, black tactical jacket and trousers, sunglasses, holster and pouches, fingerless gloves. Comes out of the **Agency van**. Role in v2: a slower, bigger, harder-to-sneak-past chaser; a takedown might need two hits or a side approach only.
- **Agency van**: keep as a landmark vehicle that spawns thugs when it spots you.
