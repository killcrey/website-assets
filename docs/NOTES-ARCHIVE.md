## Known Quirks & Critical Context (full)

- **Portal rarity is intentional** — do not "fix" the fact that portals can't spawn during a `'normal'`-anomaly state; this was explicitly confirmed by the user as by-design, not a bug.
- **`document.hidden` blocks `requestAnimationFrame`** — a backgrounded/headless test tab never advances `gameLoop`. Verify logic by calling game functions directly (`startGame()`, `checkCollisions()`, `entity.update()`, etc.), not by waiting on real frames.
- **Audio autoplay**: unlock is wired to multiple gesture types (`pointerdown`/`click`/`touchend`, each `{once:true}`) plus a persistent retry-on-every-touch fallback (`retryAudioIfStuck`) for in-app browsers (Instagram, etc.) that can silently ignore the first unlock gesture.
- **Boss/anomaly system**: three bosses exist — Octopus (`octo`, common), The Source (`source`, rare; killing it flashes the screen and drops straight into the blue-sky memory-flight level, no choice screen and no email — see the 2026-11-05 bullet below), Cobra (`cobra`, Toxic-anomaly capstone) and Super Ego (`superego`, Enemy-Zone capstone). Capstone bosses roll eligibility once when their anomaly starts (25% chance, score-gated, own cooldown) and spawn partway through if eligible.
- **Leaderboard hardening SQL is drafted but not applied** — score-range/username-format constraints were written for the user to run manually in the Supabase SQL editor; this repo/session has no service-role access to apply schema changes directly.
- No test suite exists and none is planned — verification is manual, via the Browser tools against the local static server and/or the live domain.

- **Camera closer + wall zoom cause found (2026-11-06)**: default camera distance is now `CAM_DIST_PORTRAIT = 5.0` / `CAM_DIST_LANDSCAPE = 4.6` (was 6.6 / 6.0). The real cause of the camera zooming in when you merely walk beside a building: the two side rays used for pull-in sat 0.7 m either side of the lens, so any wall within ~0.9 m of the character counted as "blocking" and pulled the whole camera in to the 1.1 m floor even though the wall was parallel to the view. They are now 0.25 m out (a near-plane-wide check). Limiter rates 7/s in, 2/s out. Verified headless: running along a building face reaches full distance (max 4.93 m) instead of staying at ~2.4 m, pop metric 0-1 in the lamp-row/building-face runs and 9 in the zig-zag (max single-frame jump 0.4 m). `playtest.mjs` `dtDrift` is still intermittently non-zero (0 / 1.65 / 2.37 in three runs) — the open E4 item in `AUDITS.md`, not camera-related.

- **Pull-in softened (2026-11-06)**: the camera is now pulled in by buildings only — low cover (`cover: true` walls: benches, dumpsters) is skipped by every camera ray (`firstHit(..., skipCover=true)`) because the lens sits ~2.5 m up and looks over them — and never closer than `CAM_MIN_DIST = 2.6` m (was 1.1, which filled the screen with the character). The trade-off is deliberate: with a building 1 m behind you the lens can sit up to ~1.6 m inside the wall box, and because the facades are single-sided planes the view from there still looks right (checked in a headless screenshot with the character's back to a building). Measured: zig-zag beside a wall 0 pops, closest camera 2.62 m (was 1.12), lamp row 1 pop; frames with the lens inside a building box rose 8 -> 18 in the wall-hugging runs (the intended cost).

- **2D logic runs on a fixed 60 steps/s clock (2026-11-06, audit B6)**: everything in the 2D game is per step (speeds, spawns, timers, the ship's 0.15 lerp), and the loop used to take one step per painted frame — so a phone drawing 30 fps (iOS Low Power Mode, a hot phone) ran the whole game at half speed, and a 60 Hz screen with tiny timestamp jitter lost ~20% of its steps. `gameLoop` now accumulates real time (`frameAcc`) and runs `runFrame()` (the old loop body, unchanged) once per `STEP_MS` owed, at most `MAX_CATCHUP_STEPS` (3) per animation frame; the backlog beyond that is dropped, so a device slower than ~20 fps slows down instead of spiralling. Only the last step of a frame paints: earlier ones run with `ctx` pointing at `nullCtx` (a do-nothing Proxy) while `realCtx` is the real canvas context — keep any new drawing going through `ctx` and don't read values back from it. 1 ms of slack in the step test keeps a 60 Hz screen's 16.6 ms frames counting as steps; screens faster than 60 Hz skip frames between steps. Measured by driving a fake `requestAnimationFrame` for 10 simulated seconds (old -> new logic steps): 30 fps 300 -> 600, 45 fps 450 -> 600, 60 Hz with +-0.3 ms jitter 470 -> 601, 20 fps 200 -> 600, 60/90/120/144 Hz unchanged at ~600; real headless rAF 60.8 steps/s, no errors; boss flash -> sky level -> 3D hand-off -> return flows still pass. Not verified on a real 30 fps phone or ProMotion iPhone.

- **3D vehicle hulls: black fringes at grazing angles fixed (2026-11-10, reported: "grazing-angle banding bothers me")**: seen on the green lowrider (and slightly on others) when looking along a car's length: black stair-step patches along the windshield, cabin and hood edges. Cause, found by collapsing each face type (top / front-back / flank) in the live scene and by dumping the hull texture: `buildHull` cuts boxes from coarse silhouette masks and textures their faces from the front/side/back pictures packed into one canvas, but those pictures are transparent outside the car and an opaque `MeshBasicMaterial` draws a transparent texel BLACK, so wherever a box face sampled empty picture (the edge of the silhouette, and above all the open cabin of a convertible, where the boxes reach into air) it came out black. Not the box steps themselves, and not the flat roof patch (removing top faces left the black). Fix in `buildHull` (`3d/index.html`): after the pictures are drawn into the hull canvas, the car's average colour (sampled every 7th pixel from the mask data `maskOf` already read) is painted UNDER them with `destination-over`, so empty texels come out car-coloured. Also: each step's top face now takes its colour from the side picture just under it (`sU(z mid, y1 - 4 px)`) instead of one flat roof patch. Cost: none measurable: 8 hulls took 156 ms total before and after (a first version that grew the edges with shifted redraws or per-pixel loops cost 1.7 s / ~40 ms per hull and was dropped). Verified in headless Edge on the lowrider, pickup, hatchback and taco cart at grazing angles: black gone, nothing else changed; 53 canvas-textured meshes along the street, no console errors. Before/after: `C:\Users\16193\game-research\graze-before.png`, `graze-after.png`. **Not verified**: a real phone; the residual blockiness of the silhouette itself (the boxes are still a stepped approximation: 30 rows), which is now body-coloured, not black.
